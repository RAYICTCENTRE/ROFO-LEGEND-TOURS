Corporate Identity & Legal Status

Rofo Legend Tours (U) Ltd is a legally registered private limited liability company, fully recognized under the laws of Uganda. The company was formally incorporated in 2013 through the Registrar of Companies and is duly certified by the Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB).

Historical Background & Heritage

Rofo Legend Tours is a heritage-driven tourism company deeply rooted in generations of experience along the Nile corridor and Lake Albert region. The company’s identity is intertwined with the history of Murchison Falls National Park, where successive generations contributed to navigation, conservation, and tourism facilitation.

The family legacy includes the historic 1954 rescue operation of author Ernest Hemingway after his plane crash near Pajao Falls (now Murchison Falls). The mission involved the SS Robert Coryndon and crew, including Okot Augustine Lukuru. Later, Rafa Oyoo Francis Okot (1947–2009), legendary ranger and conservationist, dedicated over 45 years to Uganda Wildlife Authority at Murchison Falls. Rafa’s expertise guided the BBC documentary Michael Palin's Hemingway Adventure and inspired generations. His legacy of resilience, professionalism, and passion continues to shape our vision.

Mentored by Paul Jane Goldring (founder of Wild Frontiers Uganda), the company blends African heritage knowledge with modern tourism standards — creating authentic, conservation-led experiences.
